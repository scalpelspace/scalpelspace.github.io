About

ScalpelSpace Inc. is an open-source engineering hardware startup based in Ontario, Canada.

The idea grew out of STEM journeys in high-school robotics, rocketry, Formula SAE Electric, and countless online kits—and out of the tools that were missing along the way. What started as late-night workarounds in software, embedded firmware, and PCB design became something worth building properly.

The mission: deliver open-source engineering hardware and tools that empower STEM education and accelerate rapid prototyping.

Documentation

Hardware references, pinouts, schematics, firmware guides, and worked integration examples all live on the documentation site at docs.scalpelspace.com.
